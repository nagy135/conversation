Rocketbox variants

Female_Adult_01 (Blonde), Female_Adult_02 (Cardigan), Female_Adult_03 (Short dark hair), and Female_Adult_08 (Long brown hair) from Microsoft Rocketbox, released under the MIT license.

The character uses its original facial rig and authored speech poses. Adapted for the browser by converting FBX to GLB, compressing textures and geometry, and setting up materials, framing, and lighting.

Speech animation

Wawa Lipsync by Wawa Sensei, MIT license. Uses the library's speech estimates and demo blending. Audio analysis runs locally in your browser.

Rain · Original avatar

Rain Rig (CC) Blender Foundation | studio.blender.org, licensed under CC BY 4.0. Rain preserves its original animation. Its asset was adapted by baking facial/head poses and textures, posing the ponytail, cropping the body, and compressing the model.
